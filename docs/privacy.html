Android TV HA Bridge

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Developer: Josh McNabb (@CyborgFingers) · App ID nz.mcnabb.atvhabridge

Android TV HA Bridge does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cloud. Everything happens on your own local network, between your TV and your own Home Assistant installation.

What the app does

The app runs on your Android TV / Google TV and exposes — over your local network only — what the TV is currently playing, plus the ability to control it. Your Home Assistant server connects after you approve a one-time pairing. The developer operates no servers and receives no data.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

Information handled locally (and never sent to us)

To do its job the app reads the following on the device and makes it available only to the Home Assistant instance you pair with, over your local network:

WhatWhyLeaves your network?
Active media session (title, artwork, position, state)Report what's playing (notification access)No
Android TV "Watch Next" list (READ_TV_LISTINGS)Recover show/episode titles for apps with no media metadataNo
List of installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)Let Home Assistant launch appsNo
Device status (model, network, storage, memory, volume, Wi-Fi, uptime)Optional Home Assistant sensorsNo
Navigation actions (D-pad, Back, Home)Performed only when you trigger them from Home Assistant (accessibility service)No

Communication between the TV and Home Assistant is protected by a per-device paired token and TLS encryption with certificate-fingerprint pinning. None of the above ever reaches the developer.

Data sharing

We do not share any data, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
